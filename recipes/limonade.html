What is this?

This is a fresh lemonade. A drink that is useful when the wether is so hot.

This is one of the easiest drinks ever. Prepararing it won't take you too long!

Ingredients

  • water
  • lemon
  • sugar
  • a little bit of salt if you want

Steps

  1. squeeze 2 lemons in the glass
  2. fill the glass with water
  3. Put some sugar on it
  4. a little bit of salt
  5. enjoy
