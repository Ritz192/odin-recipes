What is this?

This is a delicious sandwitch, easty to made, one of my favorites dishes actually.

You can cook it with so much differents ways. My favorite one is grilled.

Ingredients

  • Bread
  • Tomato
  • Cheese
  • Jamon
  • Mayo
  • Ketchup if you want.

Steps

  1. Open the bread
  2. Put some mayo
  3. Put chesse as much as you want
  4. Put jamon
  5. Put some tomato
  6. Put some ketchup
  7. Enjoy
